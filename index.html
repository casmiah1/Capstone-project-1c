What your BMI result
means

A BMI range of 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly considered a healthy range for adults. BMI is only one measure of health and should be considered alongside other factors, such as activity, nutrition, and medical history.

♡

Healthy eating

Healthy eating supports energy, digestion, immunity, mental clarity, and overall well-being.

↗

Regular exercise

Regular movement supports fitness, strength, heart health, and a healthy daily routine.

☾

Adequate sleep

Sleep supports concentration, emotional balance, and the body's everyday recovery.

Limitations of BMI

Although BMI is a practical screening tool, it does not tell the whole story of a person's health. Consider individual circumstances and consult a qualified health professional when interpreting results.

♀ Gender

Body composition can vary between individuals. BMI does not directly measure body fat or account for every difference in body composition.

♙ Age

Age and stage of growth matter. Children and teenagers need age- and sex-specific BMI interpretation rather than adult ranges.

♧ Muscle

BMI may classify muscular people as overweight because it cannot distinguish muscle from body fat.

○ Pregnancy

Pregnancy changes weight and body composition. BMI alone is not a complete way to assess health during pregnancy.

♙ Background

Health risks may differ among populations, so BMI should be interpreted with individual context and professional guidance.
